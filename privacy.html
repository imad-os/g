My PC – Privacy policy

Applies to the My PC app for Samsung Smart TV and its web version. Last updated: October 2026.

Summary

There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. My PC keeps a backup of what you do in it, so you can get it back after reinstalling the app or resetting the TV, and it can share your best scores in the World records. You can turn both off in Settings → Privacy & security.

Data stored on your TV

Profiles (name and colour), their settings (language, clock, background, volumes, graphics quality), saved games, calendar notes, the list of apps installed from the App Store and the top-10 score tables are saved in the app's local storage on your TV. Uninstalling the app deletes them from the TV.

Cloud backup

A copy of that data is kept in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), together with the TV model, the app version, the language and the dates the app was first and last used. The copy is filed under a code computed from your TV's own device identifier; the identifier itself is never sent, and the code cannot be turned back into it. When the app is installed again on the same TV, it uses this copy to restore your data. The owner of My PC can see these copies (to count the TVs that use the app and which apps they installed). You can stop the backup, and delete the copy, in Settings → Privacy & security.

World records

When a score is one of the 10 best of a game, the profile name and the score are sent to Firebase and shown to every My PC user in Leaderboards → World. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & security.

App Store

The App Store counts how many times each app is installed and opened. These counters are not linked to your TV.

Network use

When the app starts, it downloads update files (program code and game levels) from imad-os.github.io, and the App Store list from Firebase. The hosting providers (GitHub, Google) may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses, as described in their own privacy statements. The app works without a network connection, using the last data it downloaded.

Apps from the App Store

Apps and games from the App Store run from their own websites, inside a sandbox. They can only reach their own site and the data My PC gives them (language, volume, profile name and their own saved data). Each one has its own privacy policy.

Children

My PC is suitable for all ages. It asks for no e-mail, phone number or account. Profile names are chosen by the users; parents can pick names that are not real names, and turn off the World records.

Contact

For questions, contact the seller e-mail shown on the app's page in the Samsung TV store.
