My PC – Privacy policy

Applies to the My PC app for Samsung Smart TV and its web version. Last updated: October 2026.

Data we collect

None. My PC does not ask for, collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Data stored on your TV

Game progress, settings (language, volumes, graphics quality, background), profiles, calendar notes and the top-10 score tables, including the 3-letter initials you type, are saved only in the app's local storage on your TV. They never leave the device. You can delete them with Settings → Reset progress, and uninstalling the app deletes them too.

Network use

When the app starts, it downloads update files (program code and game levels) from imad-os.github.io. No identifier, account or personal information is sent. The hosting provider (GitHub) may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses, as described in its own privacy statement. It also reads the public list of installed apps from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), without signing in. The app works without a network connection, using the last list it downloaded.

Installed apps

Apps and games installed from the My PC installer page run from their own websites, inside a sandbox. They can only reach their own site and the data My PC gives them (language, volume, profile name and their own saved data). Each one has its own privacy policy.

Children

My PC is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

For questions, contact the seller e-mail shown on the app's page in the Samsung TV store.
