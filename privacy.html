Arcade – Privacy policy

Applies to the Arcade app for Samsung Smart TV and its web version. Last updated: October 2026.

Data we collect

None. Arcade does not ask for, collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Data stored on your TV

Game progress, settings (language, volumes, graphics quality) and the top-10 score tables, including the 3-letter initials you type, are saved only in the app's local storage on your TV. They never leave the device. You can delete them with Settings → Reset progress, and uninstalling the app deletes them too.

Network use

When the app starts, it downloads update files (program code and game levels) from imad-os.github.io. No identifier, account or personal information is sent. The hosting provider (GitHub) may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses, as described in its own privacy statement. The app works fully without a network connection.

Children

Arcade is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

For questions, contact the seller e-mail shown on the app's page in the Samsung TV store.
